PUBLIC WEBSITE

Privacy & analytics

This statement covers the public website at forkit-ai.com, not the separate local Footprint application or the authenticated forkit.dev service.

Optional visitor measurement

With your permission, the website may load Google Analytics 4 (Google LLC) to understand page views, promotion sources (LinkedIn, Hacker News, Reddit, GitHub or partner links) and safe interaction categories, including simulated reconstruction demonstrations, interest in Footprint and contact clicks. These events are not real reconstructions or installations. It uses Google's cookies and may send usage information to Google. No Google Analytics scripts or events are loaded before affirmative consent. Rejecting analytics does not prevent you using the site.

Community contribution is separate from website analytics. The new private Footprint candidate has optional local consent and payload-review controls, with its replacement collector currently disconnected. No public contribution sample size or reconstruction statistic is asserted. Previously distributed app versions may have different consent notices and network behavior. Website analytics are separate from local AI activity. No prompts, responses, source code, process logs, credentials or local Footprint evidence are included in GA4 events. The reconstruction feature contains simulated examples only.

Action Passport demo

The Action Passport demo uses fictional, simulated data only. Its search runs in your browser and nothing you type is sent to Forkit. With optional analytics consent, Forkit measures only these categories: that the demo was started, which synthetic dataset size was selected, whether a search matched the demo refund (never the search text), that the reconstruction or passport was viewed, the result of the in-browser integrity check, that the evidence package was downloaded, that the demo was completed and that a pilot link was clicked. Without consent, none of this is sent.

Private-beta interest requests

Footprint private-beta requests use an email-app handoff. The website itself does not collect, store, or transmit your email address or selected AI-tool list. If you choose to send the message, your email and chosen platform/tool categories are delivered through your own email provider to the Forkit technical mailbox for responding to your request. No website account is created, and requesting access does not enroll you in anonymous Footprint telemetry.

With separate optional website analytics consent, Forkit can measure anonymized categories such as beta CTA clicks, form starts, self-reported request-completion clicks and requested operating system. Email addresses and tool selections are never included in analytics. A self-reported click does not prove that an email was received or count as an installed user.

Your choice

You can reject or allow optional analytics and change your decision at any time.

If you withdraw consent, collection stops and first-party GA cookies are cleared on a best-effort basis. Information previously processed may remain subject to the configured GA4 retention and deletion policies.

Controller and contact

Forkit AI, Germany. Questions or privacy requests: partnership@forkit.dev.

Additional information

Google describes the operation of its analytics service at Google Privacy Policy. Before enabling the web stream, Forkit AI must finalize its legal disclosure, Google Analytics data-processing settings and retention policy as applicable.
