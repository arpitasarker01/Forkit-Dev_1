WHY TRACES ARE NOT ENOUGH

The evidence already exists. It just lives in different systems.

AGENT TRACEWhat the agent says it attempted.

Useful context, but not authoritative proof of identity, permission or downstream effect.

IAM / IDENTITYWho or what had authority.

Shows credentials and permissions, but not necessarily which business action those permissions produced.

TOOL / MCP / APIWhat crossed an execution boundary.

Shows requests and responses, but may not prove the final system-of-record change.

BUSINESS SYSTEMWhat actually changed.

ERP, CRM or cloud audit records prove effects, but often lose the original human/agent decision chain.
